## WinIsland: a Dynamic Island for Windows

A little black island at the top of your screen that unfolds into live activities.

### Highlights
- **Two looks:** a macOS-style notch flush with the top edge, or an iOS-style floating capsule. Switch any time from the top of Settings.
- **Music:** album art, seek bar, controls and a visualizer driven by your real audio output. Works with Spotify, YouTube and any app that shows Windows media controls.
- **Karaoke lyrics:** time-synced lyrics that fill in as they're sung, in the dashboard and in the island itself. Click a line to jump there.
- **Several players at once:** chips at the top of the dashboard choose whether you control Spotify, YouTube or another player.
- **Also:** volume HUD, timer, weather, CPU/RAM rings, camera and microphone indicators, notifications.
- **Light:** event-driven, about 0% CPU while idle. It hides during fullscreen games and videos.

### Install
1. Download **WinIslandSetup.exe** below.
2. Run it and pick your look, folder and preferences. It installs per-user, so no administrator rights are needed.
3. Open settings with **Ctrl + Alt + F**.

Uninstall from **Settings → Apps → Installed apps → WinIsland**.

### Requirements
- Windows 11 (x64)
- Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime (included with Windows 11)
- No .NET install needed; the app is self-contained.

### Privacy
Lyrics come from LRCLIB (free, open source). Only the song's title, artist, album and length are sent, once per song. Weather comes from Open-Meteo, and only for the city you choose. No accounts and no tracking.
