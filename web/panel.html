Music & Lyrics

Works with Spotify, YouTube and any app that shows media controls in Windows. With several players open, pick which one to control from the chips at the top of the dashboard.

Now playing

Now playingAlbum art, seek bar and playback controls.
Music pop-upDrop down with the album art, title and artist whenever the song changes.
Audio visualizerBars that move with your real sound output, sampled only while music plays.

Karaoke

Karaoke lyricsA Lyrics tab with time-synced lines that fill in as they're sung. Click a line to jump there.
Sing along in the islandWhile music plays, the island widens and shows the current line.

Lyrics come from LRCLIB, a free and open-source lyrics database. Only the song's title, artist, album and length are sent, once per song, and the answer is kept on your PC.

Weather

Shown in the idle island and the dashboard. Data from Open-Meteo (free, no account), refreshed every 30 minutes.
